# LPG Business & Staff App

Separate staff-only Flutter package. It authenticates against `/staff/auth/*`, obtains permissions and shop assignments from `/staff/me`, sends `X-Shop-Id` on shop-scoped requests, and shows the authorized shop's order queue.

Planned Android application ID: `com.perfectlpg.businessstaff`. Native Android/iOS runner projects, signup administration, catalog management, physical-cylinder selection and POS fulfillment screens are not yet generated.
